Bring us to practice

Built around how Science Olympiad actually works.

Every team is different. Some students are brand new to an event. Others already place and want to move up. We fit the help to the student, their division, and their event list.

What a practice visit looks like

We split the room so more students get help at once.

Small groups

A few students per group, working through a practice test together one question at a time.

At the whiteboard

When a question trips up a lot of people, we break it down step by step for everyone.

One-on-one

We go desk to desk, checking work and catching mistakes before they turn into habits.

What we offer

  • Event coaching

    One-on-one and small-group help on specific events, matched to each student's division and event list.

  • Study materials

    Notes, practice tests, and reference sheets for the study events.

  • Build event support

    Help designing, building, and testing devices well before competition day.

  • Practice tests

    Timed tests modeled on real competitions, so the format and pace aren't a surprise.

  • Competition prep

    Strategy sessions on pacing, picking events, and what to expect on tournament day.

  • Flexible scheduling

    Sessions that fit around school schedules and the tournament calendar.

Who it's for

Middle school (Division B) Science Olympiad teams in and around Louisville.

Students new to an event

We start with what the event actually tests and what to study first, so the first practice test isn't a wall of unfamiliar questions.

Students who already place

We go after the questions that keep costing points, and work on speed with timed tests.

Not sure what your team needs?

Tell us which events you're working on and we'll figure it out together.
