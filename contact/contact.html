FAQs

When will my application be processed?

Applications are typically processed within 1-2 weeks. If you don't hear a reply within 14 days, please email us at sciolyprep.program@gmail.com

What school will I be assigned to?

We will assign you to a school based on your availability and the needs of the schools each week.

How will I get to the other school if I don't have a car?

We will provide transportation to the schools, but you will need to provide your own ride back home.
