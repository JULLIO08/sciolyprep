How it works

You reach out

Email or call with your school, when your team practices, and the events you want help with.

We make a plan

We talk through which practices and which events make the most sense to start with.

We show up

We come to your practice and work with students in small groups and one-on-one.

Common questions

Who does the tutoring?

High schoolers who've competed in Science Olympiad themselves. We remember where we got stuck, so that's where we start.

Where do you work?

Louisville, KY. We come to middle school team practices instead of asking students to go somewhere else.

Are you replacing our coach?

No. We're extra hands, so the coach isn't the only person trying to cover dozens of students and 20+ events.

Do you work one-on-one?

Yes. Most of our time is with small groups, but we also go desk to desk and work with students one-on-one.

Which events can you help with?

Study events and build events. Tell us which ones your team needs and we'll go from there.
